OPEN CALL 2026

Show us your perspective on post-digital reality and the worlds emerging through new technologies, algorithms, systems and applications – whether real or imagined.

We are looking for projects, practices, and research-based works that engage with the theme of this year’s festival edition.

The Open Call is addressed to individuals working at the intersection of art, technology, and digital media – including those creating or researching digital culture, games, film (e.g. fiction, animation, documentary), as well as programmers and curators (the list is open).

This year’s programme is built around three interconnected thematic threads that serves as a compass, guiding us between grand narratives and global challenges on one side, and personal and whispered stories on the other.

imagining – envisioning worlds and speculating on possible scenarios for today and for the future.

mediating – understanding the tools and media that shape how we experience the world; navigating a reality of technological, cultural and social change.

belonging – seeking new and revisiting existing forms of community, coexistence and connection.

Selected participants will be invited to be part of the programme of the international INTER:AKT festival, where they will have the opportunity to present their projects to the public, connect with artists, researchers, and organisations, and join a growing network of people working at the intersection of art and technology.

APPLICATIONS

Applications can be submitted in Polish or English only via the application form.

Application deadline: 13 September 2026.

The Open Call is divided into two tracks:

TRACK 1 - on-site presentations in Katowice

This track is for projects to be presented in Katowice such as exhibitions, art installations, workshops, presentations, screenings, panel discussions, performances, VR, AR and MR projects, interactive films, innovative games, transmedia and cross-media projects, net art, and creative coding.

Both completed works and works in progress that are ready for public presentation are welcome.

Application form: https://forms.gle/gc33uzhXMaecPP4K6

TRACK 2 – online presentations

This track is for short online presentations showcasing projects, research-based-works, and prototypes related to digital and interactive practices in the broadest sense. These may include VR, AR and MR projects, interactive films, innovative games, transmedia and cross-media projects, net art, and creative coding. Both completed works and works in progress are welcome. Presentations may last up to 12 minutes.

Application form: https://forms.gle/YryMhvKb6XWJtCKeA

Additional materials should be added to the application form as links to external services (e.g. Google Drive, Dropbox, or others). For video materials, please provide links to external platforms (e.g. YouTube, Vimeo, or others), so that the files can be viewed without having to be downloaded.

Before submitting your application, please read the Open Call Guidlines.

WHAT WE OFFER?

  • curatorial and professional support – consultation and support in developing the presentation of your project, along with curatorial guidance. We can offer up to two online consultation sessions,
  • a database of available exhibition and event venues in Katowice (for on-site projects) – we are working on establishing contacts with various venues that will be available during the festival. If you are not sure where to present your project, we may be able to help you find a suitable place,
  • networking – an opportunity to meet interesting people from the art world.
  • promotion through the festival’s and partners’ communication channels.
  • a document confirming your participation in the festival, available upon request for those applying for funding to support the presentation of their project.
  • a certificate of participation in the festival,, issued after the festival.
